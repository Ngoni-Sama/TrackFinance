TrackFinance reads your EcoCash, OneMoney, Kashagi, Omari and bank SMS and turns them into budgets, spending insights and professional invoices, all on your phone.

Everything your money is doing, at a glance

No typing in every transaction. When an EcoCash SMS lands, TrackFinance logs it, files it under the right category and updates your totals.

✉

Reads money SMS

EcoCash, OneMoney, CBZ, Steward, FBC, ZB, NMB, Stanbic and BancABC. Airtime spam and network alerts are ignored.

◔

Budgets that warn you

Set a monthly limit per category and watch it fill up: green, then amber at 80%, red when you go over.

⇄

Kashagi & Omari aware

Loans show how much you've repaid and when the rest is due. Omari transfers count as savings, not spending.

▤

Daily expense log

Add cash spending by hand with a receipt photo. See each day, week or month split by category.

✎

Branded PDF invoices

Your logo, line items, VAT and EcoCash or bank details. Share by WhatsApp or email and track what's been paid.

⌂

Learns your merchants

Recategorise a payment once and TrackFinance remembers that merchant from then on.

Percentages you can read in a second

Every number that matters is a dial or a bar: how much of your income is gone, how far through the month you are, how much of a loan is repaid. If the first dial is ahead of the second, slow down.

Up and running in a minute

Install the APK and open TrackFinance.

Turn on SMS tracking and allow SMS access when Android asks.

It scans the last 6 months of money SMS, without duplicates.

Set budgets, add your business details, and send your first invoice.

Your SMS never leave your phone

Messages are read and parsed on your device. Only money SMS from known senders are used; personal messages are skipped. No account, no server, no upload. Don't want SMS access? Use manual mode.
